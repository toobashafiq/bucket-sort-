Definition: Bucket Sort sorts numbers by distributing them into buckets by value, sorting inside each bucket, then joining the buckets together in order.

🧺 Real-life idea

Think of sorting exam papers by marks: make piles for 0–25, 26–50, 51–75 and 76–100, tidy each pile, then stack the piles from lowest to highest.

The whole idea in one picture

1 Unsorted numbers
⬇ Distribute into buckets by range
2 Buckets (sorted inside)
⬇ Join the buckets together, left to right
3 Sorted numbers

✅ Fast when

Numbers are spread evenly.

⚠️ Slow when

Most numbers land in one bucket.

Which bucket?

Each bucket covers a range. With k = 5 buckets, width = 100 ÷ 5 = 20, so bucket = ⌊x ÷ 20⌋. Example: 39 ÷ 20 = 1.95 → ⌊1.95⌋ = 1 → B[1].

Time & Space Complexity

Step 1. Create k buckets → k
Step 2. Put n numbers in buckets → n
Step 3. Sort each bucket → small buckets are cheap, big buckets are expensive
Step 4. Join the buckets → n + k

The three cases (8 numbers, 8 buckets)

Best: O(n + k)
1 number per bucket. Nothing to sort.
Average: O(n + k)
Spread evenly. Buckets stay small.
Worst: O(n²)
All numbers in one bucket. Insertion Sort is slow.
